## What is Blockchain?
A blockchain is a distributed, decentralized digital ledger that records transactions across many computers.

### Key Characteristics
1. **Decentralized** - No single point of control
2. **Immutable** - Data cannot be changed once recorded
3. **Transparent** - All transactions are visible
4. **Secure** - Cryptographic protection
